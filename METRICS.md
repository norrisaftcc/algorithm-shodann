## 📈 Growth Philosophy

This leaderboard measures **improvement**, not absolute skill.

- **Velocity**: composite of coverage improvement, iteration count and complexity growth
- **Trend**: direction across the last three submissions
- **Submissions**: total PRs, because consistency matters
- **Coverage**: current coverage, shown as context and never as a ranking factor

*The citizen who grows from 0% to 30% outranks the citizen who stays at 90%.*
